轻量化密钥更新
$$ w_{new} \in_R \mathbb{Z}_n^* \implies W_A^{(i+1)} = (w_{new} \cdot G) + U_A^{(old)} \quad \text{(仅 KGC 侧)} $$ $$ H_A = \text{SM3}(\text{ENTL} || ID_A || params_{sys} || P_{pub}) $$ $$ \lambda^{(i+1)} = \text{SM3}(W_{A_x}^{(i+1)} || W_{A_y}^{(i+1)} || H_A) \pmod n $$ $$ T_A^{(i+1)} \equiv (w_{new} + \lambda^{(i+1)} \cdot ms) \pmod n $$
用户端接收部分公私钥：\(\{ W_A^{(i+1)}, T_A^{(i+1)} \}\)
$$ r_{new} \in_R \mathbb{Z}_n^* \quad \text{(仅 KGC 侧重新多项式切片)} $$ $$ c_0^{(i+1)} \equiv (ms \cdot r_{new}) \pmod n $$ $$ P_{new}(x) = c_0^{(i+1)} + \sum_{j=1}^{T} c_j^{(new)} \cdot x^j \pmod n $$ $$ M_{y}^{(i+1)} = P_{new}(M_x^{(old)}) \pmod n $$
用户端接收部分公私钥：\(\{ M_x^{(old)}, M_y^{(i+1)} \}\)
* 符号说明：\(ms\) (底层 KGC 主私钥)，\(U_A\) (用户持有的离线实体防篡改参数)，\(w_{new} / r_{new}\) (密钥更新系统在每次更新中注入的单边新随机熵)。
